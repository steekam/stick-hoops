# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First public release.

**Play**
- Six modes: Practice, Levels (ten courts), 60 Seconds, Streak, and two-player HORSE and Sabotage on one device.
- Scoring: swish, bank, off-the-wall and moving-hoop bonuses, distance points, streak multipliers.
- A three-shot tutorial on first visit; How to play in the menu.
- Four looks (Plain, Gym, Street, Notebook) and an optional dot-matrix scoreboard.
- Synthesized sound; no audio files.

**Release polish**
- Pause: Esc, P or the Pause button. Leaving the tab pauses the round, so the clock never runs while you're away.
- End-of-round cards have Share (native share sheet on phones, copy elsewhere) and Menu.
- A new personal best gets confetti, a fanfare and a "New best" stamp.
- The court always fits the screen: phones held sideways see the whole floor; header buttons go icon-only on narrow screens; the clock shows on phones too.
- Phones held upright get a hint to turn sideways.
- Installable (web app manifest, app icons) and playable offline after the first visit.
- Link previews: description, social card image and theme colour.
- Feedback button: bug reports and level or mode ideas, filed as GitHub issues.
