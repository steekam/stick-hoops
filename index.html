About Stick Hoops

Stick Hoops is a free basketball game you play in the browser. A stick figure stands on a sketched half court; you press anywhere, pull back like a slingshot and let go. The longer the pull, the harder the shot. Swishes, bank shots and long shots score extra. It works with a mouse, a touch screen or the keyboard, needs no account, and keeps working offline once you've played it.

Six ways to play

  • Practice: a full aiming guide and no pressure.
  • Levels: ten courts with walls, headwinds, tailwinds and a hoop that rides up and down.
  • 60 Seconds: score as much as you can before the buzzer. Streaks multiply your points.
  • Streak: one miss ends it, and the wind keeps rising.
  • HORSE: two players on one phone. Miss a shot your rival made and take a letter.
  • Sabotage: two players, five shots each. Makes charge power-ups to use against your rival.

Controls

Press, pull back away from the hoop and release. On a keyboard, the arrow keys set angle and power and Space shoots. Esc or P pauses.
