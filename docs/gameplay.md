# Gameplay

## Controls

| Action | Pointer or touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aim and shoot | Press on the court, pull back away from the hoop, let go. A longer pull shoots harder; a very short one cancels. | ← → angle, ↑ ↓ power, Space to shoot |
| Pause | The Pause button | Esc or P |

Leaving the tab pauses the round, so the 60 Seconds clock never runs while you're away.

## Modes

| Mode | Players | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice | 1 | Full aiming guide, no score pressure. The tutorial ends here. |
| Levels | 1 | Ten courts, each with a ball limit and a number of makes to clear it: distance, a wall, a hoop that rides up and down, headwind, tailwind, gusts. Clearing one unlocks the next. |
| 60 Seconds | 1 | Score as much as you can before the buzzer. The clock starts on your first shot. |
| Streak | 1 | One miss ends it. The wind picks up after two makes; after six the hoop starts to move. |
| HORSE | 2, one device | Make a shot and your rival has to match it from the same spot and wind. A missed match takes a letter. Spell HORSE and you lose. |
| Sabotage | 2, one device | Five shots each. A make charges a power-up to use on your rival's next shot: Gust (wind), Elevator (moving hoop) or Tight rim. Ties go to sudden death. |

## Scoring

Every make is worth 2, plus bonuses:

| Bonus | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Distance | 0 to 2, scaling from 220 px out to 560 px |
| Swish (no rim, no board) | +1 |
| Bank shot | +1 |
| Off the wall | +2 |
| Moving hoop | +1 |

In 60 Seconds, consecutive makes multiply the score: ×2 from the 3rd in a row, ×3 from the 6th,
×4 from the 10th. Personal bests are kept per mode, on this device only.

## Looks

The Look button switches the backdrop: Plain, Gym, Street or Notebook. You can also turn on a
dot-matrix scoreboard. Light and dark themes follow your system.

## Feedback

The Feedback button in the header and "Suggest a level or mode" on the menu file a bug report, an
idea or general feedback. See [deploying](deploying.md#feedback-worker) for where these go.
